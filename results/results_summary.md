## 5. Interpretation

- **No dose-response effect survived FDR correction** in either the LMM or the ANCOVA: in this sample, more Focus Bear engagement was not associated with greater improvement in ADHD symptoms or self-efficacy.
- The pre→post changes in Section 1 describe within-person change over time. With **no control group** they cannot be attributed to Focus Bear: maturation, repeated testing and regression to the mean are uncontrolled.
- **Power:** n = 65 is small, so the null dose-response results may reflect limited power as much as a true absence of effect. Focus-mode events were sparse (33 of 65 participants had none), so that dose family is especially underpowered.
